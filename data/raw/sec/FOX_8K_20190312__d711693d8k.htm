ITEM 8.01

OTHER EVENTS.

On March 12, 2019, Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc. (the “Company” or “21CF”) and The Walt Disney Company (“Disney”), in connection with Disney’s acquisition of 21CF (the “Acquisition”), announced that the deadline for holders of 21CF common stock to elect the form of consideration they wish to receive in the Acquisition will be at 5:00 p.m., Eastern Time, on March 14, 2019. 21CF expects to distribute, at approximately 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on March 19, 2019 all issued and outstanding shares of common stock of its wholly owned subsidiary, Fox Corporation, to 21CF stockholders (other than holders of the shares held by subsidiaries of 21CF) on a pro rata basis. 21CF expects that the Acquisition will become effective at 12:02 a.m. Eastern Time on March 20, 2019. A copy of the joint press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
