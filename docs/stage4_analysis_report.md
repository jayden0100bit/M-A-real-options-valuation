## 1. Synergy value

Management guidance: about **$2.0bn of run-rate cost synergies**, starting in the first year and fully realized by
**fiscal 2021**. No revenue synergy or integration cost figures were published at either valuation date.

* **Cross-check:** the B base value ($11.73/share) is close to the value implied by Disney's own advisors' disclosed ranges
  ($11.00, with pre-reform taxes and slightly lower growth). This independently supports the scale of the guidance-based model.
* **Sensitivities (B):**
  * discount rate +1pp / +2pp: $18.3bn / $15.6bn;
  * realization 50% / 75%: $10.3bn / $16.1bn;
  * integration costs at 2.0x run-rate: $20.5bn (small effect, because they are one-off);
  * synergy growth 0% / 2%: $19.0bn / $26.1bn.
* **Terminal value:** the value is dominated by the perpetuity of the run-rate savings. Durability is the key judgement.
* **Revenue synergies:** excluded. The advisors' implied value was about another $9.6/share (May) to $10.8/share (June),
  but no amounts were disclosed to model.
* **Hindsight:** with the later ~$1.5bn severance estimate, base net value is $22.3bn (B). This was not used in A or B.

## 2. Premium coverage

| Consideration vs standalone (per 21CF share, acquired business only) | Consideration | Standalone (Stage 3) | Premium | Base synergy coverage | Value created for Disney (base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: original offer at the 13 Dec 2017 close | $29.54 | $29.08 | $0.46 (≈ $0.9bn) | 24.2x | +$19.9bn |
| B: original offer at the 30 May 2018 close | $27.44 | $26.22 | $1.22 (≈ $2.3bn) | 9.6x | +$19.7bn |
| B: **amended offer $38.00** (subsequent, 20 Jun 2018) | $38.00 | $26.22 | **$11.78 (≈ $22.0bn)** | **1.00x** (bear 0.58x, bull 1.53x, weighted 1.03x) | **−$0.1bn** (bear −$9.1bn, bull +$11.7bn) |

* **Original offer:** the premium over our standalone value was small, so the synergies left most of their value with
  Disney's shareholders. The walk-away price (standalone + 100% of base synergies) was about $40.30 (A) and $37.95 (B).
* **Amended $38 offer:** essentially equal to the B walk-away price. With base synergies Disney paid away **all**
  of the synergy value: coverage is 1.00x and value creation is about zero. In the bear case the synergies cover only 58% of
  the premium. The bidding contest with Comcast transferred the synergy value to 21CF's shareholders.
* **Caveat:** "premium" here is over our intrinsic standalone value, which differs from the market premium (Stage 3:
  54% over the unaffected price, but 21CF's price also included New Fox). With 21CF's own, more optimistic forecasts
  (Stage 3: $35.69 standalone), the amended premium is only $2.31 a share and is comfortably covered.

## 3. Accretion / dilution (Disney fiscal years; close 20 Mar 2019)

| | FY2020 | FY2021 | FY2022 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disney standalone EPS (estimate from street forecasts) | $8.77 | $9.48 | $9.98 |
| **Base: pro forma adjusted EPS** (ex purchase accounting and integration costs) | $8.32 (**−5.1%**) | $9.34 (**−1.4%**) | $9.64 (−3.4%) |
| Base: pro forma GAAP-basis EPS | $6.82 (−22.3%) | $8.10 (−14.6%) | $8.48 (−15.0%) |
| RSNs retained, adjusted | −2.0% | **+1.4%** | −1.1% |
| No synergies, adjusted | −10.0% | −10.5% | −12.1% |
| Original all-stock (515m shares), adjusted | −13.6% | −10.4% | −12.2% |
| Original all-stock + $20bn buybacks, adjusted | −8.6% | −4.9% | −6.7% |
| *Reported anchor (Disney 8-K/A pro forma, GAAP, no synergies):* FY2018 $8.36 → $5.94 | | **−29.0%** | |

The base case uses the closing structure: $35.7bn cash (net new debt after acquired-cash and RSN-proceeds repayment, at
3.3%), 306.8m new shares, $2.7bn a year of purchase-accounting amortization and base synergies.

* **Management's claim:** accretive before purchase accounting in the second fiscal year, i.e. FY2021. Our base is
  **slightly dilutive (−1.4%)**. It is accretive if the RSNs had been kept (+1.4%), if synergies reach 125% of guidance
  (+0.8%), or if more of the price were paid in cash. The claim is plausible but on a knife edge. Management's version also
  included buybacks, which Disney cancelled in March 2019.
* **Synergies are essential:** without them, adjusted EPS is about 10–12% dilutive in every year.
* **Financing mix matters most:** the 50/50 cash/stock amendment is far less dilutive than the original all-stock deal
  (−1.4% vs −10.4% in FY2021), because 3.3% debt costs less than issuing shares at an earnings yield of about 8%.
* **On a GAAP basis the deal is clearly dilutive** (−15% to −22%), mostly because of $2.7bn a year of purchase-accounting
  amortization. Disney's own pro forma showed −29% for FY2018, without synergies and on an EPS base inflated by tax reform.

## 4. Key assumptions (all in the assumption / input CSVs)
* **Synergies:** $2.0bn run-rate (guidance); phase-in 10% / 50% / 100% over FY2019–21; integration costs 1.0x run-rate;
  synergy growth 1%; standalone WACC as discount rate; probability weights 25/50/25. All except the run-rate and timing are
  estimates.
* **Accretion/dilution:**
  * Disney's standalone earnings come from the "Disney street forecasts", which are analyst-based and not management's.
  * RemainCo's earnings come from Disney's forecasts for RemainCo.
  * Interest, NCI and share count are held flat (estimate).
  * The 3.3% rate on new debt is Disney's own pro forma assumption.
  * $2.7bn a year of amortization is derived from the 10-K; 22% tax rate.

## 5. Limitations
1. Revenue synergies are not modelled, and neither are the dis-synergies from moving content to Disney's own services.
   Both were material in management's long-term thinking.
2. Synergy phasing, integration costs and probabilities are estimates. Only the run-rate and the 2021 target are guidance.
3. Some of the "savings" may be 21CF corporate costs that would otherwise have moved to New Fox ($275–350m a year), so
   synergies incremental to the standalone value could be overstated.
4. The accretion model holds interest, NCI, shares and amortization flat. It does not model Hulu consolidation, the tax on
   the RSN sale, or DTC investment, and it pre-dates COVID-19. It tests management's claim; it does not predict reported EPS.
5. The Disney standalone EPS base is built from analysts' segment forecasts approved for the advisors' use, not from
   management EPS guidance.
6. The original-deal figures use the closing-date RemainCo perimeter for comparability. Under the original terms the
   Sky stake would still have been held.
